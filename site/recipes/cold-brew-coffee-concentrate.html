Cold Brew Coffee Concentrate

Toddy system · 1:4 ratio · coarse grind

Ingredients at a glance
Whole beans coarse ground 340g (12oz)
Water 1:4 ratio — ½ kettle hot + 1 full kettle room temp ~1360g
  • Setup Insert felt filter into Toddy container. Seat the rubber stopper firmly in the drain hole.
  • Bloom Add all 340g ground coffee. Pour ½ kettle of just-boiled water over the grounds. Do not stir. Wait 1 minute.
  • Fill Pour 1 full kettle of room temperature water over the grounds in a circular motion. Do not stir. Gently press any floating grounds down with the back of a spoon.
  • Steep Cover and steep 12–24 hrs in the refrigerator.
  • Drain Remove stopper over a glass carafe. Allow to drain fully, about 20–30 min. Do not press or squeeze the grounds. Keeps refrigerated up to 2 weeks.

Serve diluted 1:1 with water or milk for a standard strength cup.
