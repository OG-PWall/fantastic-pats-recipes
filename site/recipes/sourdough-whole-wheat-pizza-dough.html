Sourdough Whole Wheat Pizza Dough

1 × ~670g ball · one 12–14″ pizza · ~91% hydration · halved recipe · adjust start time to fit your day · original ↗

Ingredients at a glance
Autolyse flour / water 300g / 270g
Mix starter / salt 100g / 8g

Water 80–90°F. Hydration runs high (~91% with starter) — Vitamix-milled, sifted flour absorbs less, so hold back ~30g water at autolyse and add only if the dough feels dry.

Day 1
9:00 AM
Autolyse
300g whole wheat flour + 270g warm water (80–90°F). Mix until no dry flour remains.
Cover, rest 1 hr.
10:00 AM
Mix dough
Add 100g sourdough starter (active for best rise; discard works too) + 8g kosher salt. Mix by hand until fully incorporated.
Cover, rest 15 min.
10:15 AM
Stretch & fold #1
First of 6 sets, 15 min apart. Wet hands, grab an edge, fold over the center, rotate bowl 90°, repeat 4–8× per set.
10:30 AM
Stretch & fold #2
10:45 AM
Stretch & fold #3
11:00 AM
Stretch & fold #4
11:15 AM
Stretch & fold #5
11:30 AM
Stretch & fold #6 — then bulk
Last set. Cover and leave in a warm spot (70–75°F).
11:30 AM – 4:00 PM
Bulk fermentation
4–6 hrs at 70–75°F. Done when doubled in size with visible surface bubbles.
~4:00 PM
Shape
Form into a rough rectangle, fold edges toward the center, seal, flip seam-side down, and shape into a smooth round ball.
4:00 PM
Proof
Rest ball 20–30 min on a floured surface, covered with plastic wrap.
Use immediately, or refrigerate up to 3 days for deeper flavor.
When ready
Stretch & bake
Stretch to 12–14″, top, and bake on a stone or steel preheated as hot as the oven goes (500°F+). Source gives dough only — bake to your setup.
